The Wan2.2 I2V weights redistributed in this image have been modified by
skyphusion-labs as follows:

  1. Numeric recast from fp32 to bf16.
  2. Numeric recast from bf16 to fp8 (float8_e4m3fn), produced for the baked
     image as the "Wan-AI/Wan2.2-I2V-A14B-Diffusers-fp8" variant.

These changes are precision/quantization recasts of the tensor values only.
No change was made to the model architecture, layer topology, configuration,
or to any accompanying source code. The recasts preserve the original
diffusers layout and are intended to reduce VRAM footprint and load time.
