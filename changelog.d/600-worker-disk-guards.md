### fix(harness): a worker whose disk cannot hold the job fails that one job by number and is replaced

A worker's model cache persists across jobs on purpose. On the old 80 GB disk, one i2v-capable job's Wan pull (about 114 GiB) filled it, and every later job on that worker failed within seconds on ENOSPC (vivijure-backend#600). Now a model pull is sized first (`rclone size` over exactly what the copy would copy) and fails its job with the numbers when it cannot fit with the job floor left free. A job also fails by number before it starts when free disk is below the floor (4 GiB, `VJ_MIN_FREE_GB`). Either failure, or an ENOSPC raised anywhere in a job, is returned with RunPod's `refresh_worker`, so the SDK stops that worker and RunPod replaces it instead of it eating the queue. Every job's `started`, `complete` and `error` progress events now carry `disk_free_gb`. The endpoint disk is 300 GB (docs/runpod-endpoint-config.md has the arithmetic).
