**Remove: `keyframe.pass_b_gaze` and `keyframe.plate_gaze` (vivijure-cf#1442)**

Both knobs were added to measure the workbench gaze class: under per-region plate text, side-by-side faces look into the lens. Neither helped on the live endpoint. `pass_b_gaze: auto` (1.1.22) held 3 of 6 and `plate_gaze: auto` (1.1.23) held 2 of 6, against PRF3's 3 of 6. In one still the plate drew both people looking down and Pass B still turned both to the lens. So the gaze is set by Pass B's identity adapter, not by either prompt. Both were off by default and never forwarded by the studio. This reverts #651 and #653: `src/`, `tests/` and `docs/configuration.md` are byte-identical to 1.1.21 (`852eac6`), so every reuse hash is the one 1.1.21 computed. A job that still sends either key has it ignored, like any unknown key.
